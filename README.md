Welcome to the **IE² Lab**, led by **Ritesh Kumar** at **TCG CREST**. We are a multidisciplinary team dedicated to accelerating the transition to sustainable energy through the fusion of Artificial Intelligence (AI), multiscale simulations, and chemistry.

---

## 🚀 Our Mission
We bridge the gap between atomistic insights and real-world energy devices. Our goal is to discover the next generation of materials for **batteries, fuel cells, and supercapacitors** by building "self-driving" laboratories and interpretable AI frameworks.

---

## 🧪 Core Research Pillars

### 🤖 1. AI-driven Discovery
We develop big data-driven approaches frameworks to screen millions of chemical candidates.
*   **Recent Highlight**: Reduced experimental screening by 80% for battery electrolytes using Bayesian optimization (*Nat. Commun.* 2025).

### ⚛️ 2. Multiscale modeling
Using MD, DFT, and Continuum modeling, we investigate the fundamental physics of interfaces.
*   **Key Discovery**: Uncovered how water clustering in aprotic media controls CO₂ reduction activity (*JACS* 2025).

### 🛠️ 3. Scientific tools & Agents
We build tools that automate the "boring" parts of science—from autonomous data extraction from papers to CV-based crystal structure conversion.
*   **Projects**: `AtomBridge`, `curAItor-agent`.
